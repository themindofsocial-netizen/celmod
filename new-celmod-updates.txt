|CelMod| New Update List:

	2024:
	 - 04/23/24: Added commands !landdeathmatch and !landgravity to change the respective settings for your land.
		 - Added more command options for !alpha (all), !color (all, hud) and !delete (all, land).
	 - 08/11/24: Added command !renderfx to change the RenderFX on entities.
		 - Added command history HUD and the ablitiy to spawn a prop without the !spawn command. Ex. !blastdoor3, /blastdoor3

	2025:
	 - 04/24/25: Added !balance, !buy, !sell, !setbalance to the server.
		 - More economy features are being worked on. !landskin has been added but is currently broken.
	 - 04/26/25: Added !emitter as an alias for !effect. Added !switch to switch the side of the screen the HUD is on.
		 - Added !stack and !stackinfo for easier building.
	 - 05/01/25: Added +grab, +copy, !skin, !copy and !paste.
		 - Update rainbow and sm_fadecolor to use HSV to RGB for a better and more consistant color.
	 - 05/08/25: Added a blacklist to blacklist props without altering the prop list.
	 - 05/30/25: Added !ladder and !light to spawn in ladder and light cels. Updated +grab to +move.
		 - Added bits for weapons, ammo, and chargers.
			 - !ammo
			 - !ammocrate
			 - !charger
			 - !weapon
	 - 06/03/25: Started work on control bits, spawn one with !button/v_button and link it to a cel using !link/v_link.
		 - Fixed !skin next/prev to automatically scroll through all skins on a prop.
	 - 06/09/25: Added commands !replace/v_replace to replace the model of a prop and !owner/v_owner to print the owner of the entity.
		 - Updated !lock/v_lock and !unlock/v_unlock to be their own commands instead of being hidden in !freeze/!unfreeze.
		 - Added command !drop/v_drop to drop entities to the floor.
	 - 11/11/26: Added emblems. Press E on an emblem you own to enable it, then hold your gravity gun to use its power.
		 - Other players see the emblem in your hands. Emblems: fire, speed, jump, blizzard, health, gold.
		 - Added hats. Press E on a hat you own to wear it.
		 - Added !emblem, !hat and !emblems/!hats/!inventory to spawn and list the items you own.
		 - Added emblem drops: crates fall from the sky, and any crate inside your land when the timer ends is yours.
		 - Added reward pickups for mazes and parkour (!reward, !delreward) and !giveitem, !emblemdrop for admins.
		 - A free emblem can appear in your build when you load it, once you have been building for a few days.
		 - Fixed !buy and !sell. The price and owner now show on the HUD when looking at something for sale.
		 - Added !give to give an entity to another player and !trade to swap entities with another player.
	 - 11/12/26: Emblems now match the original set: 30 emblems using the emblem model, each with its own color, skin and power.
		 - Added !thirdperson and !firstperson so you can see your own emblem and hat.
		 - Added the yearhat, delaware, partyhat and santahat hats.
		 - Added !cl to drop money, !moveto, !turn, !noclip, !fire, !weps, !stuck, !clear, !landslay and !landkick.
		 - Added fire, cland and landskin to the shop.
	 - 11/13/26: Emblem shots now have their own effects: flames, ice, water, energy bolts, smoke, orbs, lasers and rainbow beams.
		 - Added !undo, !hide, !show, !weld, !release, !landban, !landunban, !message and !sound. !landkick now keeps a player out for 10 seconds.
		 - Saving and loading builds is more reliable and reports when a build cannot be written.
		 - Added a standalone emblem editor web page in webapp/emblem-editor.
	 - 11/14/26: Builds now load into your own land, in the same place they were saved, and only if the whole build fits.
		 - Saving only includes entities whose origin is inside your land.
		 - Added !landcolor, !palette, !model and !teleport.
	 - 11/15/26: The fire emblem is now a flamethrower, and right click throws a fireball that explodes on impact.
		 - Blizzard and Energy also have right click attacks (ice storm, big shot).
		 - Held emblems now point forwards. Hats find the head on more player models.
		 - !emblems, !hats and !inventory show everything you own in one list.
		 - Added !tune for admins to line up held emblems and hats while in game.
	 - 11/16/26: Teleports, message cels, sound cels, welds and buttons with their links are now saved with builds.
		 - The Power Booth appears on every map. Bring it 5 of the same emblem to make one big emblem.
		 - Added event areas and restricted areas for admins (!zone, !delzone, !zones).
		 - Added celweps: weapon cels you own and press E on to take the weapon (!weapon, !weapons).
		 - Added the Guess Booth (!guess) and auctions above it (!auction, !bid).
		 - Added buyable player models (!playermodels, !buymodel, !playermodel).
		 - Emblem drop crates are tinted with their emblem's color. Drops are admin triggered by default.
	 - 11/17/26: Celweps can be real, separate weapons with the Custom Guns plugin: axe, fire sword, ice blade, power blade, pickaxe and shovel.
		 - The shovel can dig up Black and White emblems.
		 - Emblems reworked: Solar blinds players facing you, the Errors fire errors (25/50/75 damage), Smog makes a coughing smoke cloud,
		   Chaos raises a burning antlion hill, Demon raises towers of dark power and shakes screens, Nuke drops a headcrab canister.
		 - Added the Orange emblem (double speed). Cloud now jumps higher with lower gravity.
		 - The Power Booth shows a menu: 2 Errors make a Blue Error, 2 Blue make a Green, and 5 of any emblem make a big one.
		 - Player models can be admin only, and admins can give them with !givemodel.
	 - 11/18/26: 58 new emblems have been added to the emblem pool, from Magma and Glacier to Rocket, Ghost, Vampire and Diamond.
	 - 11/19/26: The Power Booth now appears floating somewhere random in the map, and the Guess Booth on open ground somewhere random, instead of in the spawn rooms.
		 - Emblems are switched off when you join the server.
	 - 11/20/26: The Nuke is a falling canister again and no longer posts in chat. Smog is one quieter cloud at a time.
		 - The Fire emblem shows its flames, and its fireball is now a real projectile that explodes on impact.
		 - The Power Booth is the HEV suit case. The Guess Booth is bigger, shows all three props, and Dr. Kleiner greets you there.
		 - The shovel digs up junk, money boxes, and now and then a Black or White emblem.
	 - 11/21/26: The Power Booth now works with the emblems you bring to it: spawn them, carry them up to the booth, then press E on it.
	 - 11/21/26: Added the website database integration (celmod-database): live server data, player profiles and web shop orders.
	 - 11/22/26: The Power Booth and Guess Booth no longer appear near each other. Dr. Kleiner wears a traffic cone.
		 - The Guess Booth message is shown quietly at the top of the screen.
		 - The Power Booth tells you how many emblems you brought and how many each upgrade needs; new emblems land under it.
		 - New celweps: Sunflower, Saw Gun, Melon Launcher and Blade Bow.
	 - 11/23/26: The Guess Booth shows three props again, is bigger, and Kleiner has his cone. The Power Booth hides as far from spawns and the Guess Booth as it can.
	 - 11/24/26: Guess Booth props, Kleiner's cone and hats show again (they were scaled, and scaled models do not show). The Guess Booth is a little smaller.
		 - The Power Booth hides in tucked away spots out of sight of spawns and the Guess Booth.
	 - 11/25/26: Celweps are shown in your hand and their name appears when you switch to one. Admins can line up held emblems, celweps and hats in game with !nudge and !tune; it is saved.
	 - 11/26/26: The Guess Booth props are shown again.
		 - The Guess Booth props and the auction item slowly spin.
	 - 11/27/26: The Power Booth no longer uses a menu: bring 5 of an emblem for the big one, or 2 Errors for a Blue Error, and press E.
	 - 11/28/26: Celweps are no longer carried over when you rejoin, and no name pops up when you pull one out.
		 - Dug up junk cannot be broken: carry it to a trash can for a few cleers. Trash cans are placed around every map.
	 - 11/29/26: One celwep at a time: press E on a celwep to take it, on another to swap, on the same one to put it back. The Custom Guns weapon wheel is gone.
	 - 11/30/26: Trash cans are no longer placed around the map; any trash can or dumpster prop takes junk.
	 - 12/01/26: The Error emblems (and every flying shot) now fly to where you aim, and the Nuke's canister really falls.
		 - Emblem drops are emblem boxes in the emblem's skin: the box you hold with your gravity gun when the map changes is yours.
		 - Money drops and dug up money use the cleers money box.
		 - New Phantom emblem: hold right click to phase through walls.
		 - Tactics level: earn experience by playing, building and fighting. The HUD shows Money, Tactics Lvl and an EXP bar. !tacticlvl, !tacticxp.
	 - 12/02/26: !landskin <1-26> covers the floor of your land with a land skin (bought once in the shop). !landskin off removes it.
	 - 12/03/26: Land, money, emblem and camera fixes, and every emblem has a new look.
		 - !coop now works: players in a co-op land can't hurt each other (missing messages added too).
		 - !gravity takes a multiplier from 0.05 to 4 (or game units, e.g. 300 = half), never negative, and applies at once.
		 - Dropped cleers are owned cleers boxes with the right skin (CL, K, M, B). Only the owner opens one (E); !give hands it over. The HUD shows the owner and amount.
		 - Emblem drops fall one at a time (cm_drop_delay). Looking at an emblem box shows its emblem; boxes left in your land at map change are yours, and E opens one inside your land.
		 - Boxes fall back to stock models when their .vvd/.vtx/.phy files are missing, so they can always be picked up.
		 - !thirdperson keeps following the player's aim (cm_thirdperson_aimfix). Hats go back on after a player model change.
		 - New admin commands: !givemoney and !takemoney <player> <amount>.
		 - The Power Booth works once per map, then overloads and explodes (harmlessly) and is gone until the next map. It is hidden somewhere different and harder to reach each map.
		 - Every emblem has its own shot style and its own aura while held (cm_emblem_aura).
	 - 12/04/26: The Guess Booth has a fixed spot per map: stand there and type !setguessbooth (saved in data/celmod/guessbooth.txt). !delguessbooth removes it. Maps without a spot have no Guess Booth.
		 - The Power Booth hides much better: only spots no spawn point or Guess Booth can see, far from spawns and boxed in, chosen at random, never near last time's spot. It is also fainter (cm_powerbooth_alpha).
	 - 12/05/26: Event emblems: !eventemblem <emblem> places one where you aim (end of a maze or parkour). Press E to get it; each player can only ever earn each event emblem once. They glow and the HUD says what they give. !delreward removes one.
		 - Event and restricted areas now also stop phasing, blinking, portals and teleport pads from getting players in, and emblems give no movement help (speed, sprint, gravity, jump, feather) inside event areas.
		 - Event items are fixed objects (nothing can move, pick up or break them) for emblems, hats and celweps: !eventitem <emblem/hat/weapon> <name>. Every player can press E on one to claim it, once and only once per item. Reward pickups are fixed objects too.
		 - !eventitem link <emblem/hat/weapon> <name> makes a map's own object (a prop, button, door or other brush entity) the claimable event item. It is saved by Hammer ID and comes back every time the map loads; !delreward unlinks it.
	 - 12/06/26: The Power Booth now hangs as high as each map goes (just under the ceiling or sky), preferably tucked into a corner and out of sight of spawns, picked at random from the best few spots.
	 - 12/07/26: Players inside a co-op land show the co-op skull above their head, and players inside a deathmatch land show the deathmatch skull. Everyone can see them, from anywhere (cm_land_skull_size in units, default 16, sized from the texture itself; cm_land_skull_height).
	 - 12/08/26: Tapping the walk key (left Alt) changes your walking speed: normal, slow, very slow, super slow, fast, and round again. Fast is capped to normal inside event areas.
		 - !block <player> stops you from entering that player's land: you are stopped at the edge. !block <player> again (or !unblock <player>) undoes it; !block on its own lists who you blocked. Remembered between visits.
	 - 12/09/26: !music <song> [speed 50-200] spawns a music cel (E plays the song, E again stops it; saved with builds). !celmusic lists the songs, from the "Music" section of data/celmod/sounds.txt.
	 - 12/10/26: Build teams: !addteam <player> lets them build in your land and edit the props in it (as the original Team Build did). !delteam <player> removes them; !delteam on its own lists your team. Teams are remembered between visits.
		 - Teammates' props stay theirs (their prop limit; only the real owner can sell, give or trade them), but the land owner's !save includes everything the team built in the land, and !load gives it all to the land owner.
		 - !speed turns the walk key speed change on or off (handy when Alt-Tabbing). Turning it off puts you back to normal speed. Remembered between visits.
	 - 12/11/26: Guess Booth spots are now saved one file per map in data/celmod/guessbooth/, which CelMod updates never overwrite (the release used to ship an empty guessbooth.txt that wiped saved spots). Old guessbooth.txt spots are still read. !setguessbooth says so if the spot could not be written.
	 - 12/12/26: The Power Booth now looks across the whole map (not just near the spawn points), no longer always picks the single tallest area, and is chosen at random from the better third of the spots found. It never returns within an eighth of the map's size of last time's spot.
	 - 12/13/26: Hats now really sit on players' heads (they were being placed near the middle of the map). The same fix applies to emblems and celweps shown in hand, and Dr. Kleiner's traffic cone is now fixed to his head.
	 - 12/14/26: New shop props: CelMod's own models are now for sale all year (album, album3, cake, coreball, coreballsmall, delaware, hoopie, piano, pineapple, sign, sign1, sotora, spprop, flatscreen, tvscreen).
		 - Seasonal shops open around each holiday (dates in the "Seasons" section of shop.txt): the Halloween Shop (gift, jackolantern, jackolanternbig, pumpkin1-3), the Christmas Shop (candycane, present, santahat, xmastree), the New Year Shop (partyhat, yearhat) and the Clock Day Shop (sbc, the Strawberry Clock, around August 15th). Bought seasonal props can be spawned all year. !seasons lists them; players are told when one is open.
		 - Shop models (and the materials they use) are sent to players automatically, read from the model itself. A model without a .phy file spawns as a solid prop that does not move instead of failing.
		 - The button cel uses the models/dav0r/buttons/switch.mdl switch when the server has it.
		 - Announcements made in the website's admin panel show in chat as |CelMod| <message> (once, repeated every few minutes, and/or when players join).
	 - 12/15/26: Fixed hats, Dr. Kleiner's traffic cone and other plugin-made props disappearing: they were renamed before spawning, so the game deleted any whose model is a physics-only prop.
		 - Hats and held emblems/celweps can be resized in game: !nudge hat scale 0.1 (or -0.1), or a scale at the end of !tune. Saved in data/celmod/tuning.txt with the position.
	 - 12/16/26: Celweps fixed. The hidden weapon behind each celwep no longer fires bullets of its own, so melee celweps only swing.
		 - The Melon Launcher fires melons that roll for 2 seconds and then explode; the Saw Gun fires spinning saw blades; the Blade Bow fires swords that fly and stick where they land.
		 - The Fire Sword sets players on fire (5 seconds), the Ice Blade freezes them solid (2 seconds), and the Power Blade hits hard (85 damage). Each hit has its own effect.
